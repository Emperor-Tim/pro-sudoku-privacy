Pro Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Pro Sudoku is made by Timothy Mironenko. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. It doesn't need an account, and I don't sell your information.

Information kept on your device

Your game in progress, your statistics and your settings are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version of Pro Sudoku shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location
  • how you interact with the ads
  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Pro Sudoku can track you. You can change your answer at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, but they're less personalised.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings in the app, under Privacy Choices.
  • No ads: buying Pro Sudoku Plus removes all ads. Once ads are gone, the app doesn't load the advertising service at all.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro Sudoku Plus purchases. I only receive confirmation that you've bought it, never your payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

Pro Sudoku isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your information, email tim_m@outlook.com.
